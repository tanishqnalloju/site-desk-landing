1. **Custom site** — copy and layout for their offer; semantic HTML; fast static or edge delivery.
2. **SEO basics** — unique title + meta, H1/H2 structure, canonical, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, Open Graph / Twitter cards.

4. **Hosting** — on the customer’s domain or a subdomain they control; DNS documented honestly. Domain registration is separate.
